# vocnet Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to vocnet and its related iOS client application (collectively, the “App”). Please read this Policy carefully before using the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide core functionality, comply with legal obligations, or with your consent.

### 1.1 Account Information

- **Required information**: email/username, password, or third‑party login identifiers (such as an OAuth2 user ID).
- **Purpose**: to create and manage your account, authenticate your login, help you recover access, and protect your account security.

### 1.2 Learning and Usage Data

- Vocabulary data you create (e.g., wordbooks, words, sample sentences, tags);
- Study and review records, such as learning progress, mastery level, and review schedules;
- Practice results, statistics, and related learning metrics.

**Purpose**: to provide vocabulary management, memory tracking, review scheduling, and learning analytics so that the App can offer personalized recommendations and progress reports.

### 1.3 Device and Log Information

- Device model, operating system version, and App version;
- Request time, accessed URLs/paths, error logs, and crash reports;
- IP address, which may be processed in an anonymized or pseudonymized way for security and anti‑abuse purposes.

**Purpose**: to ensure the security and stable operation of the service, diagnose issues, and improve performance.

### 1.4 What We Do Not Collect

- We do **not** intentionally collect your contacts, photos, messages, or other unrelated personal content;
- We do **not** activate your microphone, camera, contacts, or similar sensitive permissions without your explicit consent.

If a future feature requires access to such permissions, we will request your explicit consent through the system permission dialog and explain the purpose before any access occurs.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. Provide, maintain, and improve core features of the App, such as creating wordbooks, scheduling reviews, and generating study statistics;
2. Manage accounts and protect security, including identity verification, login protection, and anomaly detection;
3. Diagnose and fix bugs, and improve user experience based on error and performance data;
4. Comply with applicable laws and regulations, including responding to legitimate requests from regulatory or judicial authorities.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the App without your consent.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

1. **Location**: Your data is stored on servers configured by us or on servers used by your self‑hosted/third‑party vocnet deployment, depending on how the service is deployed.
2. **Retention**: We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. When you request account deletion or when the data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize your information, unless we are legally required to retain it longer.
3. **Security Measures**: We use reasonable technical and organizational measures such as access control, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), and role‑based permissions to help protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

If you deploy the open‑source vocnet software yourself, you are responsible for securing your own server and environment.

## 4. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure of Information

### 4.1 Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following situations:

- When it is necessary to use service providers to deliver certain features (for example, cloud hosting, email delivery, or analytics), in which case we contractually require them to protect your data and use it only for the agreed‑upon purposes;
- When we have obtained your explicit consent;
- When required by applicable law, regulation, or a valid request from a court or other competent authority.

### 4.2 Transfer

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale that results in the transfer of personal information, we will require the new entity to be bound by this Privacy Policy or seek your consent to a new policy when required by law.

### 4.3 Public Disclosure

We do not publicly disclose your personal information except:

- When required to comply with legal obligations; or
- When you have given us your explicit consent to do so.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable laws, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information:

1. **Access and correction**: You can view and update your profile, wordbooks, and learning content within the App.
2. **Deletion**: You can delete certain learning data in the App. If you want to delete your entire account and associated data, you may contact us using the methods described below.
3. **Withdraw consent**: For processing activities based on your consent, you may withdraw consent at any time, for example by disabling certain features, revoking system permissions, or contacting us.
4. **Account deletion**: You may request deletion of your account. After your account is deleted, we will stop providing services to you and will handle your personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

To protect your privacy and security, we may need to verify your identity before processing your requests.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is primarily intended for users with full legal capacity. If you are a minor under the laws of your jurisdiction, you should use the App only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete such information as soon as reasonably possible.

## 7. Third‑Party Services

The App may integrate third‑party services (for example, third‑party login providers or crash reporting tools). These services are operated by third parties, and their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Before integrating such services, we assess their compliance and strive to ensure they follow relevant laws, regulations, and industry best practices.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Depending on server deployment, CDN usage, or backup strategies, your data may be transferred and processed in countries or regions outside your place of residence. In such cases, we will comply with applicable data protection laws and implement appropriate safeguards.

For self‑hosted deployments of the open‑source vocnet software, cross‑border data flows depend on where you deploy your servers and from where the App is accessed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example, to reflect changes to our services or to comply with new legal requirements. If we make material changes, we will notify you by appropriate means, such as through the App or on our website.

If you do not agree with the updated Policy, you should stop using the App and may request account deletion. Your continued use of the App after the effective date of the updated Policy will constitute your acceptance of the changes.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, you can contact us via:

- Project homepage: <https://github.com/eslsoft/vocnet>
- Issues or contact information listed in the GitHub repository

We will review and respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
